# 英文 · Pangram 与音素平衡

## 一、字母全包含 Pangram（快速冒烟测试用）

> 注：pangram 只覆盖 26 个字母，**不**覆盖音素，仅作"服务通、能出声"的冒烟测试。

1. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
2. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.
3. How vexingly quick daft zebras jump!
4. The five boxing wizards jump quickly.
5. Jim quickly realized that the beautiful gowns are expensive.

## 二、Stella Passage（音素覆盖，购物清单式，自然口语）

Please call Stella. Ask her to bring these things with her from the store: Six spoons of fresh snow peas, five thick slabs of blue cheese, and maybe a snack for her brother Bob. We also need a small plastic snake and a big toy frog for the kids. She can scoop these things into three red bags, and we will go meet her Wednesday at the train station.

> 覆盖大多数美式英语辅音、元音和辅音连缀；约 20 秒，最常用于口音/克隆样本。缺失 /ʒ/、/ɔɪ/、/aʊ/，需配合下方 Rainbow 或 Harvard 补足。

## 三、Rainbow Passage（叙事式音素平衡，SLP 标准样本）

When the sunlight strikes raindrops in the air, they act as a prism and form a rainbow. The rainbow is a division of white light into many beautiful colors. These take the shape of a long round arch, with its path high above, and its two ends apparently beyond the horizon.

> 涵盖几乎所有美式英语音素，适合评估自然语流中的元音分布、连缀与韵律。

## 四、数字 / 日期 / 标点专项

1. The GDP grew by five point two percent this quarter.
2. The meeting is scheduled for October twenty-second at nine thirty a.m.
3. Call me at one three eight, zero one two three, four five six seven.
4. What time is it? — It's half past three; let's go!
5. He bought apples, bananas, and a box of chocolate (imported).
6. Temperatures dropped to twelve below zero; wind reached force six.
