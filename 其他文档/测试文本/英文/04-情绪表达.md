# 英文 · Emotion Expressions（7 categories）

Aligned with the 7 emotion labels used by the ModelScope GPT-SoVITS collection:
neutral / happy / angry / sad / fearful / surprised / disgusted.
Each line is semantically suited to that emotion — usable as emotion-labeled reference audio,
or read against the matching IndexTTS-2.5 `emo_vector`.

> Text alone only carries the *meaning* of emotion. To make synthesized speech actually sound
> emotional: (1) swap in an emotion-labeled reference audio (GPT-SoVITS), or (2) use
> `emo_vector` / `emo_text` (IndexTTS-2.5). OmniVoice has no emotion axis.

## Neutral (calm narration)
1. According to the forecast, tomorrow will be cloudy with a high of twenty-six degrees.
2. The meeting is set for three p.m. in conference room two; please be on time.
3. This is an ordinary record; there is nothing particularly special about it.

## Happy
1. Yes! We actually did it — that's absolutely amazing!
2. Hahaha, look at his face, I'm dying of laughter!
3. What a rare good day — my whole mood just brightened up.

## Angry
1. How could you do this to me! I called you a friend, and you stabbed me in the back!
2. Enough! I don't want to hear your excuses anymore — get out!
3. We absolutely cannot let this slide; someone has to answer for it.

## Sad
1. We said we'd go together… why, why am I the only one left?
2. The tears wouldn't stop, and I didn't even have the strength to cry out loud.
3. Some goodbyes turn out to be forever, after all.

## Fearful
1. D-don't come any closer… please, just stay away…
2. What was that sound? In the dark, my heart was about to leap out of my chest.
3. Run! Something is right behind us — don't look back!

## Surprised
1. Wh—what?! What did you just say, did I hear that right?!
2. Good heavens, how — how is this even possible?!
3. Wait, you're telling me that's true?! I'm completely stunned.

## Disgusted
1. Ugh, that fake smile of yours makes me sick just looking at it.
2. Get away from me — that smell on you is unbearable.
3. You'd actually do something like this? The more I think about it, the more repulsive it is.
