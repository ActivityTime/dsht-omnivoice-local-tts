# 英文 · 日常对话

适合做克隆参考音频（自然口语，3–10 秒录制）。

## Casual chat
1. Hey, you're here early today.
2. I just made some tea, want to sit for a while?
3. Got any plans for the weekend? Maybe we could go out.
4. This place is actually pretty nice, we should come back.
5. It's getting a bit chilly outside, grab a jacket.

## Narration
1. Let me tell you something interesting — I ran into an old friend yesterday.
2. Back when we were kids, we'd always run down to the river to catch tadpoles.
3. I've read that book three times, and each time it meant something new.
4. He never says it out loud, but he remembers everything, you know.
5. The story begins a long, long time ago, in a quiet little town.

## Character-flavored (anime / companion style)
1. Hey, don't walk so fast — wait up, will you!
2. Hmph, it's not like I was worried about you or anything. Just happened to be heading this way.
3. Leave it to me — something this small won't slow me down.
4. Thanks for staying with me this whole time. Really… it means a lot.
5. No matter what happens, I'll be on your side.
